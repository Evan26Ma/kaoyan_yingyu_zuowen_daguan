逻辑关系

 顺接关系

1) 并列词

and, and also, or, either…or…, neither…nor…, not only…but also…,

likewise, similarly, equally, in the same way, that is (to say), as well as, much the same as.

2) 递进词

also, then, besides, additionally, in addition, furthermore, moreover, what’s more, indeed, even, in effect, in fact

 相反关系

1) 转折词

but, however, yet, contrarily, on the contrary, by contrast, unfortunately, in fact, in reality,

actually, on the other hand

2) 让步词

although, though, even though, even if, nevertheless, despite, in spite of, regardless of, anyway, anyhow, even

3) 对比词

while, whereas, rather than.., instead of, not…but…

result=implication=consequence=effect=so…that…=such…that…

2)目的词

in order that.., for, in order to, so that, lest, for fear that, in case

3) 条件词

if, only if, unless, otherwise, as soon as, as long as, in case, suppose that, supposing that, provided that, providing that, with (the help of), when, whenever

 举例关系

 时间关系
